## Admissions at Greenfield Institute of Engineering
Greenfield Institute of Engineering offers B.Tech programmes in Mechanical, Civil and Chemical Engineering. Admissions are based on the state entrance test. The institute campus is spread over 40 acres and has a cricket ground, two hostels and a central cafeteria.
## Fees at Greenfield Institute of Engineering
The annual tuition fee at Greenfield Institute of Engineering is fixed by the state fee committee. Hostel fees are charged separately and the mess is operated by an external contractor.
