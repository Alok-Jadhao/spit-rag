## Campus «  Sardar Patel Institute of Technology
## Campus
The institute is located in 47 acres of green campus at Andheri (W), the fastest growing suburb of Mumbai. The campus also houses other Bhavan’s Institutions of great repute namely Bhavan’s College (the arts, commerce and science college), Sardar Patel College of Engineering, S.P. Jain Institute of Management and Research, a management institute.
## Cultural Activities
The students of Sardar Patel Institute of Technology are known for their organizational skills as they take charge of a spate of intra and intercollegiate events.
Udaan has been a professionally organized college festival and stands as one of the best college festivals hosted by an engineering college. It serves as an ideal platform for students all over the city to display their skills and talent through a wide range of literary, fine art, performing arts and technical competitions.
