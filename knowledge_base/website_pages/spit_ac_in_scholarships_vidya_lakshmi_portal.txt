## Vidya Lakshmi Portal
National Securities Depository Limited (NSDSL) e-Governance Infrastructure Ltd., has developed Vidya Lakshmi Portal. This portal has facilities of education loan, scholarship and other student friendly facilities through one link. The portal is gateway to Banks for education loan & also has linkages with National e-Scholarship Portal (NeSP).
40 banks have been registered so far and 71 loan schemes have been uploaded in Vidya Lakshmi Portal.
